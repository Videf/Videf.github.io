Masquerade

Itch.io

About

You have been invited to a grand masquerade. Among the guests, there is one intruder. Find them, and unmask them before they complete their objectives. Sever their strings. This game was made together with 3 artists/animators in 48 hours for the Global Game Jam. Since we all have artistic experience, the focus of this game jam was to create an interesting visual experience rather than a "fun" game.

Info

  • Made in: 2026

  • Role: Programmer, Artist

  • Group size: 4

  • Time frame: 48 Hours

  • Engine: Unity (C#)

What I Worked On

  • - Many of the pixel-art textures
  • - All gameplay programming and NPC AI
  • - Level building

Process

My role in this project was mainly leading the project, developing all gameplay systems, and providing pixel-art and artistic technical guidance. Our main artistic vision for this game was for it to simulate the look of a 2D pixel art game. This meant that we used no in-engine lighting, and instead provided this through texturing. To achieve the pixelated look, we downscaled the render output to a lower resolution. This had an unintended side-effect of blurring colors together and creating new ones that didn't exist in our color pallette. While this was unwanted for our art-style, it worked for the gameplay, obscuring farther areas of the map and creating an air of mystery.
In the end, the game ended up very simple, with a lot of janky parts (and bugs that I wish I had time to fix!), especially how the NPCs collide with each other. However, while the gameplay is imperfect, I'm very happy how this project turned out considering the time limit.
