About

'You Oar Me' is a boat-racing game where you compete against your toughest rival - yourself. This project was made during a 48 hour game-jam with the theme: 'You Or Me'.
The most challenging part of this project was the fact that it was the team's first time using the Godot game engine. Despite our unfamiliarity with the engine, we managed to finish the project under the very short time-span.
The game was made together with another programmer, and a 3D-artist.

Info

  • Made in: 2024

  • Role: Programmer

  • Group size: 3

  • Time frame: 48 Hours

  • Engine: Godot (C#)
